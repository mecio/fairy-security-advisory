# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in fAIry Security Advisory to be respectful, safe, and
constructive. Contributors and maintainers are expected to treat one another
with professionalism regardless of experience, identity, background, or point
of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful outcomes directly.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Protect private environment and security information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.
- Correct mistakes transparently and help leave the project better documented.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate
disclosure of private information, spam, impersonation, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable. Publishing another person's credentials,
security findings, private paths, or production data is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **gucw@proton.me**. Include only the
information needed to understand the incident and do not publish sensitive
evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the project and its community. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, without a guaranteed
response time.
